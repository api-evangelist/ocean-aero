# Ocean Aero

> Ocean Aero is a maritime robotics manufacturer in Gulfport, Mississippi. It designs, builds and
> operates the Triton, an environmentally powered Autonomous Underwater and Surface Vehicle (AUSV)
> that sails on the surface and submerges to collect data above and below the waterline and relay it
> back to shore. Ocean Aero sells vehicles, pre-packaged and custom payloads, and mission services to
> defense, ocean research and offshore energy customers.

## API status

- Ocean Aero publishes **no public API**, no developer portal, no API reference, no SDK, no MCP
  server and no machine-readable contract (no OpenAPI, AsyncAPI, GraphQL SDL, Protobuf or WSDL).
- Triton fleets are operated through Ocean Aero's own proprietary mission-control GUI (waypoint and
  payload management across multiple vehicles); an "Ares Autonomy Stack" is announced as coming soon.
  Neither is documented as a programmable interface for third parties.
- Probed 2026-08-26: every `/.well-known/*` path, `/llms.txt`, `/openapi.json`, `/swagger.json` and
  `/api-docs` on www.oceanaero.com returned 404; api., developer., docs., portal. and data.
  subdomains do not resolve. There is no Ocean Aero GitHub organization.
- If you need Triton data or integration, the documented route is the contact form, not an API.
